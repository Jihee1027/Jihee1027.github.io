Root causes from line data

I started from the defect trend, not the loudest complaint.

Work

Defect investigation on a battery electrode line.

Problem

Defect-related stops were costing production time.

Action

Analyzed defect trends in SPC and MES data, investigated root causes with Process and Equipment, and shared a daily defect report across Process, Equipment and Production.

Result

Defect-related downtime fell about 86% over two months, measured at the line level.
