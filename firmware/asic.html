ASIC design · Purdue ECE 337 · Team of three

AHB Neural Network Accelerator

  • SystemVerilog
  • AHB
  • QuestaSim
  • Design Compiler

A small neural-network accelerator. Software writes weights and inputs over the AHB bus, an 8×8 grid of multiply-accumulate units does the math, and an activation stage shapes the results.

My partThe compute core: the 8×8 systolic array with 64 pipelined processing elements, and the activation stage with four modes. I verified each block, then the top level, and helped debug integration. Teammates built the AHB interface, controller, SRAM buffer and top level.

64 PEsin the 8×8 weight-stationary array.
4 modesReLU, Leaky ReLU, binary and identity, selected per lane.

What the tests covered

Array

Weights land in the right positions, staggered inputs, multiply and add in 8-bit floating point, reset in the middle of an operation, and the done pulse arriving with the data.

Activation

All four modes on every lane, including negative zero, which the sign check treats as zero.

Top level

Identity and full tests with bias, a 128-input vector, and error cases: AHB overrun, NaN and infinity.
