Embedded projects

Two smaller projects: a display driver where memory was the constraint, and a balloon payload that had to work on its first and only flight.

01

Color and distance sensor robot

ECE 362, Microprocessor Systems and Interfacing · April 2026 · Team of 4. A robot that finds a target color and stops 2 inches away.

−73%RAM use, from 52.2% to 13.9% (273.9 KB to 72.9 KB), after moving to one shared display buffer and partial redraw.
40 MHzSPI to the TFT display, driven from embedded C on an RP2350.

My part

I wrote the SPI TFT display driver and the four UI screens in embedded C. The screens now share one display buffer and redraw only what changed, which is where the RAM saving came from.

The course behind it

The labs covered register-level GPIO, interrupts, timers, ADC with DMA, PWM and UART on the RP2350.

02

Edge of Space balloon payload

University of Iowa, Edge of Space Academy · July 2024 · Embedded systems project lead, team of five.

What we built

A payload launched to about 30 km to measure cosmic-ray flux, with high-voltage boards, a Geiger–Müller tube, sensors and telemetry on Arduino and Raspberry Pi.

Testing before launch

A payload gets one flight. I soldered, debugged boards, validated wiring and ran test-and-redesign cycles to improve reliability before launch.

Data acquisition

I wrote the Python and C data acquisition code on Linux.
