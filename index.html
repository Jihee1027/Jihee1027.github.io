Why TSMC

At Purdue I took chip design classes where a design only has to work in a simulator. My internship was on the manufacturing side, and it changed how I think about that.

A design is only real once it is built

No matter how good a design is, it does not exist in the real world until someone can manufacture it, repeatedly and at yield. I saw that on a production line, where the process decides whether the idea survives.

TSMC makes that happen

TSMC is where the most advanced designs become real chips. I want to be one of the people who make that possible, close to the process where it is decided.

What I bring

I read the whole line, change one thing on purpose, and let downstream results decide. I learned a new line quickly, worked nights and weekends, and I have been studying transistors and the process flow on my own.
