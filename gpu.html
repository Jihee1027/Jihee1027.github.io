Purdue SoCET GPU · Sep 2026 to present

GPU Vertex Stage

  • SystemVerilog
  • FPGA
  • Fixed point
  • Pipelining

The vertex stage of a GPU datapath, in fixed point. It transforms vertices, divides by w, and converts normalized coordinates into screen space. The work is in progress.

Problem

A vertex stage has to transform vertices, divide by w and convert to screen space, one vertex after another, in fixed point and inside an FPGA's resource and clock budget.

Data

Design targets of 125 MHz, 10M vertices per second and 60 FPS; a 24-bit Q16.8 format; RTL test results.

Action

Split the work into transform, perspective-divide and screen-space modules, then built the perspective-divide prototype and tested it.

Result

The prototype passes 8 RTL tests and produces back-to-back outputs in simulation. The other modules are still in progress.

01

What it is

Separate modules for 4×4 model-view-projection transforms, perspective division and screen-space conversion, using 24-bit Q16.8 fixed-point values.

02

Where it stands

A perspective-divide prototype passes 8 RTL tests and produces back-to-back outputs at one per cycle in simulation. The design targets are 125 MHz, 10M vertices per second and 60 FPS.

03

What I have to balance

Numerical precision, FPGA resource usage, clock frequency and pipeline throughput. Improving one usually costs another.
