Experience

Two internships in high-volume manufacturing

Holland, Michigan. A battery electrode manufacturing line, seen first through its vision inspection systems and then through its laser notching process. In both I worked on how process conditions, equipment behavior and inspection and production data connect. That is the way of working I want to bring to semiconductor process integration.

May–Aug 2026

Process Technology Engineering

Laser notching: following variation across process steps to where it began, a DOE for a stable process window, a 4M qualified change judged on downstream evidence, and two physical fixes I designed. Over a two-month period, defect-related downtime on a key production line I supported dropped about 86%.

What the two internships have in common

The tools were different. The pattern was the same.

Connect the steps

In both roles I looked at how a condition in one step showed up in a later one, and did not stop at the step that raised the alarm.

Localize with data

I put Spotfire and SPC data, vision footage, equipment logs and downstream feedback side by side to decide where a problem began.

Test a defined change, then verify

DOE, controlled trials and a 4M qualification in Process Technology. Recipe tuning checked against production images and the next production data in Vision.

Public summaries. Recipe values, line identifiers, process limits and production counts are left out on purpose.
