Two internships in high-volume manufacturing

Work covered defect investigation, recipe tuning, equipment troubleshooting, and controlled change qualification, with results checked against production and downstream quality data.

Investigated variation across laser notching steps, supported DOE and controlled 4M change qualification, and designed two physical improvements. What the two internships have in common

Both roles called for the same approach: connect the process steps, use data to narrow the cause, and verify the change.

Connect the steps

Traced how conditions in one step affected later steps, looking beyond the point where a defect became visible.

Narrow the cause with data

Compared Spotfire and SPC data with vision footage, equipment logs, and downstream feedback to narrow the source of defects.

Test a defined change, then verify

Supported DOE, controlled trials, and 4M qualification in Process Technology. Verified inspection recipe changes against production images and subsequent data in Vision Engineering.
